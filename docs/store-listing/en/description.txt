MrW Lyrics finds the lyrics of the song you are playing and shows them line by line as the music goes. No sign-up, no ads, no fiddly setup: play your music and the lyrics appear.

It works with Spotify, YouTube in your browser, Windows Media Player and any other app that shows up in the Windows media controls. It reads the song from Windows and never uses your microphone. When a video is uploaded under someone else's name and no source has its lyrics, the app can listen to the player for a few seconds and ask Shazam for the real song name. Only a fingerprint of the sound is sent, once per song, and you can turn this off.

With synced lyrics, the line being sung is highlighted and the window scrolls along. Karaoke focus keeps only the current line and the lines around it on screen. If you like, the line can fill from left to right as it is sung.

Lyrics-only mode turns the window fully transparent so the lyrics float on your desktop. Clicks that miss the letters go through to the window behind, so the lyrics stay on screen while you work, play or watch videos without getting in the way. Click a line to bring the controls back.

Make it yours: font, size, colours for the current and other lines, shadow or glow, background opacity, always on top. Or pick a ready-made style: Classic, Karaoke yellow, Neon or Plain. If the lyrics run a little ahead of or behind the song, shift the timing in half-second steps.

Lyrics you find are saved to your Music\Lyrics folder as .lrc or .txt files, so replays need no internet. You can drop your own .lrc files there too.

Lyrics are looked up from online sources such as LrcLib, Musixmatch, Genius and lyrics.ovh. Only the song's title, artist, album and length are sent, plus a sound fingerprint to Shazam when recognition is needed. There is no account, and no usage data is collected.

The interface is in English and Turkish and follows your Windows language.
